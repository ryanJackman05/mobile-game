# plan for icon
The current plan for the icon of the game is a cartoonish image of the front of the player's car, and impact hit effect, and a disembodied zombie arm flying away from the car (no blood shown, only red background) This may be stylised into a streetsign style or minimalist symbols instead of a drawing.

# feature graphic and screenshots
Feature graphic will consist of a image of the player car sailing over a jump, toward the viewer. There will be a zombie being hit by the car, and a zombie in the foreground, with impact impending.

There will be a screenshot of at least these three moments of gameplay:
- The car driving straight, with some zombies in the distance.
- The car making a hard turn as a zombie holds onto the side, another zombie mid-jump flying at the car
- The car making a mid-air jump over a hazard

# plus short (max 80 characters) and long description drafts. 
## Short
**"Who will last longer: The vicious zombie horde, or your gasoline?**

**You are alone on the road, save for your trusty car, and the hundreds of zombies in the way. You must drive to live in this tense endless runner.**

**Make tight turns, and navigate road obstacles, and fend off the ravenous horde of zombies."**

## Long
Ride or Die:

In this game, you drive a car along a seemingly endless highway, traversing obstacles, dodging or ramming enemies with your car, and constantly on the search for more fuel, upgrades, and repairs.

Features:
- Immersive car physics & daring obstacles
- Various zombies trying to attack you at all times
- Power ups and pick up items to assist in your run
- High scores and run stats

The control scheme is simple and intuitive, with the left and right sides of the screen dedicated to controlling the steering of your car. Where you're going you don't need brakes. There is an optional gyro steering mode.

How far can you make it? Try to beat your high score and drive further into the apocalypse...
 
*Official specs: app icon 512×512, 32-bit PNG (with alpha), max 1024 KB; feature graphic 1024×500, JPEG or 24-bit PNG (no alpha).*
