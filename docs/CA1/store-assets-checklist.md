# plan for icon
The current plan for the icon of the game is a cartoonish image of the front of the player's car, and impact hit effect, and a disembodied zombie arm flying away from the car (no blood shown, only red background) This may be stylised into a streetsign style or minimalist symbols instead of a drawing.

# feature graphic and screenshots
Feature graphic will consist of a image of the player car sailing over a jump, toward the viewer. There will be a zombie being hit by the car, and a zombie in the foreground, with impact impending.

There will be a screenshot of at least these three moments of gameplay:
- The car driving straight, with some zombies in the distance.
- The car making a hard turn as a zombie holds onto the side, another zombie mid-jump flying at the car
- The car making a mid-air jump over a hazard
