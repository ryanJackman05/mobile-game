# Description Drafts
## Short
**"Who will last longer: The vicious zombie horde, or your gasoline?**

**You are alone on the road, save for your trusty car, and the hundreds of zombies in the way. You must drive to live in this tense endless runner.**

**Make tight turns, and navigate road obstacles, and fend off the ravenous horde of zombies."**

## Long
Ride or Die:

In this game, you drive a car along a seemingly endless highway, traversing obstacles, dodging or ramming enemies with your car, and constantly on the search for more fuel, upgrades, and repairs.

Features:
- Immersive car physics & daring obstacles
- Various zombies trying to attack you at all times
- Power ups and pick up items to assist in your run
- High scores and run stats

The control scheme is simple and intuitive, with the left and right sides of the screen dedicated to controlling the steering of your car. Where you're going you don't need brakes. There is an optional gyro steering mode.

How far can you make it? Try to beat your high score and drive further into the apocalypse...

No ads
No In-App purchases
No online play
No personal data recorded
